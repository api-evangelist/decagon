Decagon integrated its agentic AI solution with Five9's contact center platform, now available on the Five9 CX Marketplace, letting enterprises deploy production-ready AI voice agents on existing Five9 infrastructure without replacement while maintaining human escalation. Joint customers including Wealthsimple, Chime, and Oura are already using the combined solution.
